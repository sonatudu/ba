Ba user manual

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Private room for two

We cannot see any user's data. The people who run Ba cannot read your chat, moods, memories, notes, or anything else in a room. That content is encrypted on the device. We have no way to open it for any user.

This guide lists every main screen, button, and gesture. Shared data is encrypted on the device. There are no ads and no public profiles.

Why Ba exists

Ba is not meant to replace WhatsApp, Instagram, or any other everyday app. Those tools already do chat media, groups, and the public internet well. Ba does not try to copy them.

That is why there are no image albums, video messages, or voice notes here. WhatsApp and similar apps already cover that. Adding the same features would only make Ba louder and less itself.

Ba’s purpose is to hold the more personal, private pieces of one relationship in one quiet place: days together, a poke, daily habits, a map of where you each are, to-dos for Us / Ba / Ma, good and bad notes of a day, memories, periods, family, and a short chat that stays in this room. It should feel like a shared home, not another feed.

Only two people enter a room, with a room id and code. There are no contacts lists, no status for the world, and no growth features. The people who run Ba cannot see any user's data. Keep WhatsApp (or whatever you already use) for photos, voice, and talking with everyone else. Keep Ba for the two of you.

Getting in

Anyone can make a private room for two, or join one. Each room has a room id and a numeric code you choose. Share both only with one other person.

Create a room
Enter Your name, Partner's name, and Name of your relationship (shown under Together on Home). Set a 6-digit code and tap Create. The person who creates the room is the first name. Settings then shows the room id to share.
Join a room
Enter the room id and code, then tap Open. The two names appear only after that. Pick yours, then tap Open again. A room id is not asked for the original personal room.
Who you are
Remembered on this phone. Both people see the same room. Periods stay on one side of the pair.
Set a code / Code
At least 6 digits. The people who run Ba cannot read your journal even with this code; it only opens the room.
Back
Returns to Create / Join.

Home

Home is the hub. The phone Back button returns here from other screens (and closes nested Periods/Memories editors first).

Days together
Shown under your relationship name once a start date is set in Settings.
Daily quote
A quiet line fetched for the calendar day. Not a button.
Poke (large tile with the time)
Sends a poke. Your phone vibrates. The partner’s phone vibrates only — no banner, no sound. The time on the tile is the last poke.
Chat, Routine, Daily, Where, To Do, Overview, Memories, Periods, Family
Open that section. A dot or highlight can mean unread chat or new activity.
Chat — long press
Offers Clear chat for the whole thread (with a confirm).
Settings (gear, bottom)
Opens Settings.

Chat

A small thread for this room only — text, not a WhatsApp replacement. There are no image, video, or voice-note buttons on purpose.

Opens on the latest message. The keyboard stays open after send on the phone.

Message field
Type here. On a computer, Enter sends and Shift+Enter makes a new line. On the phone, Enter is a new line; use Send. Tapping the field lifts the bar so it sits above the keyboard.
Send (paper-plane)
Sends the message without dismissing the keyboard.
↓ jump
Appears if you scroll up. Jumps to the newest messages.
Long-press a message
Copy, Edit (your messages), Delete.
Reply bar
If you are replying, the quoted preview shows above the field. The × cancels the reply.
Calls
Incoming call overlay: Accept or End. Chat notifications can show a banner; poke never does.

Routine

A customizable weekly timetable and schedule planner for both partners. Each person in the room has their own timetable and notes, end-to-end encrypted and synced between your devices.

Partner Switch
Toggle whose timetable you are looking at, using your names at the top.
Pencil on Weekly Schedule
Turns on schedule edit mode. Then you see Edit days, Edit slots, and Save. Slots cannot be changed until this pencil is on.
Subjects & Courses
Subjects are listed here (and as text inside timetable cells). The list has no delete control until you tap this section’s pencil. Then you can + Add Subject, rename fields, Delete a row, and Save.
Placing a class
Tap the Weekly Schedule pencil, then tap a table slot. Type the class name, or scroll the subject list (one name per row) and tap one. Set span (1 to 4) and Save.
Edit days
Check or uncheck any day of the week, or use the presets (Mon – Fri, Mon – Sat, All 7 Days), then save.
Edit slots
Customize daily time slots (e.g. 09:00–10:00). Rows follow your slots.
Weekly Notes
Tap the pencil to edit, then Save. Save is hidden until you edit.
Courses & Mess Meals (Room Presets)
Existing personal accounts (such as BA-OURS) preserve their detailed campus course lists and hostel mess menus with full editing and adding capabilities.

Daily

Shared habits with columns for each partner. Each task can belong to Both, or either partner. Percentages and the graph use that person’s own task count (done ÷ their tasks).

Partner progress (n/m)
Completed over that person’s total for the selected day.
Day strip
Jump to a day this month. Sundays are marked in red. Swipe the strip sideways.
Calendar icon
Jump to another date (not in the future).
Completion graph
Ranges: Today, Week, Month, Year, Overall. Two lines representing each partner's completion rate.
Partner ticks on a row
Mark that person done for that task. Empty dashed circles mean the task is not theirs.
Edit
Opens add/rename/reorder. New rooms start with no tasks — add your own. Time-of-day cycles Morning → Afternoon → Evening → Night. Whose task cycles Both and each person’s name. Drag the handle to reorder inside a slot. Long-press a row for Rename, Delete, or Keep. A task added today is counted from today onward, not on earlier days. Percentages use each person’s own tasks.
Add a daily task
Type the name, pick time of day and whose task it is. Enter adds while staying in Edit. Done saves and leaves Edit.

Where

Map of the two of you. Location is optional (Settings → Share location). Each person has one pin. If the same person is signed in on more than one phone, the pin is the latest active device (last used or last location sent), not merely the last login.

Partner locate buttons
Fly the map to that person’s pin and follow it.
Compass
Reset north.
N
Natural / satellite-style map. Streets stay underneath if satellite tiles miss.
P
Political / street map. Switches instantly without reloading the map.
Full screen
Expand or exit the map.
Pinch / drag
Zoom and pan. Tiles stay visible at high zoom on both N and P.

Memories

New rooms start with a clean memories list for the couple to add their own dates and stories. + is a small plus on the right of the saved / coming up line at the top — you do not scroll to the bottom to start a new one.

+
Small plus on the right of “0 saved” / “1 coming up”. Opens the composer: Date, Repeat yearly, title, story. Done saves the memory.
Coming up
Yearly or dated memories in the next 45 days.
All
Grouped by year (and Yearly). Long-press a card for Edit, Duplicate, yearly repeat, Copy, and Delete.
Edit
Opens from that long-press menu. Done / Delete are on the editor.

Periods

Cycle tracker. Summary, calendar, and Record period. Fertile window, next period, days between periods, and period length use averages of logged cycles, not only the last month.

Summary

Period start, fertile window, next period, days between periods, period length. Day count appears only while a period is still on. After it has ended, the day number is hidden until the next period starts.

Calendar

Swipe left / right
Change month (neighbour month peeks while you drag).
A day cell
Sets the date for Record period. Colours: period, predicted period, fertile, today.

Record period

Logged day by day, like medicine courses — not a single start/end range.

Date
The day you are recording.
Status
Still on = still bleeding that day. Ended = last day of this period.
Menstrual flow
Light / Medium / Heavy.
Symptoms
Tap Symptoms to show the list. Tick options, add notes, and use the pencil then Save to add or remove symptom names.
Save / Update
Writes that day. History is month buttons; open a month for daily rows until Ended, then a month summary. Long-press: Edit, Last day (if still on), Delete.

Meprate

Shown only in the original personal room. Track prescribed Meprate courses day by day.

Date
The day of this log.
Status
Still on / Ended (end of the course for that month).
Taken / Not taken
If not taken, a reason is required.
Save
Daily history until Ended, then a month summary. Long-press: Edit or Delete.

Gap

Shown only in the original personal room. Days from Meprate course end to the next period start (Month, Period start, Gap).

Family

Two-sided family tree. Use + Add Member. Where to add, Family side, and Gender open as a list in the middle of the screen (the relationship is only for placement — it is not drawn on the card). Cards show the name, and nickname or birthday only when those values exist. Tap the face to toggle gender. Long-press a card to remove it. Pinch with two fingers to zoom; drag to pan. Each couple is joined by a line between their faces. Children sit under that couple. The drop starts at the exact midpoint of the parents’ line and goes only to their children, not into a spouse’s bar. Shared children sit under the couple.

Settings

Sun / moon
Day theme or night theme.
Room id
Shown after you create or join. Copy shares the id so the other person can Join. Also share your room code (not shown here).
Names
Your name, Partner's name, and the relationship name (Together on Home). Tap the pencil to edit, then Save. Save is hidden until you edit.
Relationship start date
Sets the home “days together” count. Calendar picker.
Share location
On: Where can show your pin. Off: location is not shared. Optional.
Notifications
On: chat and calls can show a banner. Poke still only vibrates.

Download data as PDF
Saves a readable PDF of notes, memories, tasks, habits, periods, and chat on this device (share sheet on the phone if the browser allows it).
Session & Chat persistence
Your login session remains active indefinitely on your phone without unexpected automatic termination. Chat messages are never automatically pruned or deleted.
Log out
Leaves the room on this device. Data in the room stays for the other person until they delete the account.
Delete account
Opens a Room code field. Enter the same code used to open Ba, then tap Delete. Wrong codes are rejected. Cancel closes the form without deleting. This closes the shared room.

Gestures that show up in several places

  • Phone Back — close the current screen or nested editor; from Home, leave the app.
  • Long-press — menus on tasks, period history, memories, family cards, and chat messages.
  • Pinch — zoom the family tree with two fingers.
  • In-app calendar — tap the date control; change month with ‹ › or by swiping; tap a day.
  • Rename / Delete — long-press menus; To Do has no Keep.
