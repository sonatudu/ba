Delete your Ba account

App: Ba (com.sonatudu.ba)

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Ba and sign in to your room.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm with your room code.

That removes your username, login, and pending requests for your side. If you are in the shared room, that room is closed for the couple.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email tudus0602@gmail.com with subject Ba account deletion request. Include your Play account email and which identity you used (Ba or Ma) so we can process the request.
