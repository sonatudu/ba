Ba privacy policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026 · App: Ba (com.sonatudu.ba)

Ba is a private room for two people. There are no ads, no public profiles, and no sale of personal data.

Who we are

Ba is operated by the developer of this app (Sona Tudu). For privacy questions, contact tudus0602@gmail.com.

What Ba stores

When you open a room, Ba may store account checks (such as usernames and password hashes), session tokens, and encrypted room content (chat, notes, memories, to-dos, periods, routines, and similar shared data). Shared content is encrypted on your device before it is sent to the server. The server keeps scrambled ciphertext and login checks; we cannot read your memories or chat plaintext.

Anyone with your invite and one of the two passwords can enter the room. Do not share the invite publicly.

Location

Location is optional. Ba only uses location when you turn on Share location (or use Where features that need it). Then the app may collect approximate or precise location from your device so you and your partner can see shared pins on the map.

Location is not required to install or use the rest of Ba. You can deny permission or turn sharing off at any time in Settings. The app does not need continuous background location to work for non-map features.

Device identifiers

Ba may keep a local device id so each phone in the room can be told apart for presence and location sharing. This is not used for advertising.

How long data is kept

Room data stays while the shared room exists. Sessions expire after inactivity. If the hosting service is reset without durable storage, server-side data may be lost; keep local backups of anything important outside the app if you need them.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. That removes your username, login, and pending requests for your side. If you are in the shared room, that room is closed for the couple. Your partner’s separate account record may remain until they delete theirs.

Children

Ba is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when features or hosting change. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
