Ba privacy

Ba is a private room for two people. There are no ads and no public profiles.

Each couple gets a random invite code. Letters, dates, moods, and memories are encrypted on your device before they are stored. The server keeps only scrambled data and password checks. Other couples cannot open your room. We cannot read your memories.

Anyone with your invite and one of the two passwords can enter the room. Do not post the invite in public.

Chat in the room is encrypted on your device before it is stored. Only the two people in that room can read the messages.

You can delete your account in the app. That removes your username, login, and pending requests. If you are in a room, that shared room is closed. Your partner’s account stays.
