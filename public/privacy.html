Ba privacy policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026 · App: Ba (com.sonatudu.ba)

We cannot see any user's data. The people who run Ba cannot read your chat, moods, memories, notes, or anything else in a room. That content is encrypted on the device. We have no way to open it for any user.

Ba is a private room for two people (Ba and Ma). Anyone can create a room or join one with a room id and code. There are no ads, no public profiles, and no sale of personal data. A user manual describes the screens in the app.

Who we are

Ba is operated by the developer of this app (Sona Tudu). For privacy questions, contact tudus0602@gmail.com.

How you enter

You create a room (choose a numeric code and Ba or Ma) or join with a room id plus that code. Your identity (Ba or Ma) is stored on the phone so you are not asked every time. Do not share the room id or code publicly. Anyone with both can enter the room as one of the two people.

What Ba stores

On your device (encrypted before upload): shared room content such as chat, overview notes, memories, to-dos, daily habits, periods (including Meprate logs), family tree, routines, and similar journal data. This is encrypted on the device. The people who run Ba cannot see that content for any user. The server keeps ciphertext and cannot read that plaintext.

On the server (needed to run the room): session tokens, a device id, who is signed in (Ba or Ma), optional live location pins when sharing is on, optional push notification tokens, presence/typing for chat, and short-lived call signaling. These are not part of the encrypted journal blob.

Local settings on the phone can include theme, location-sharing choice, notification choice, and a last map pin used to draw Where quickly.

Location

Location is optional. Ba only uses it when you turn on Share location in Settings (Where needs it to show your pin). Then the app may collect approximate or precise location so you and your partner can each see one pin.

If the same person is signed in on more than one device, Ba shows the pin from the latest active device (last used or last location sent), not merely the last login. Location is not required to install or use the rest of Ba. You can deny permission or turn sharing off at any time. The rest of the app does not need background location.

Notifications

Notifications are optional (Settings → Notifications). If you turn them on, the phone may register a Firebase Cloud Messaging token with the Ba server so the partner can be alerted. Chat and calls may show a banner. Poke only vibrates; it does not show a notification. Tokens are stored per device and are not used for advertising.

Calls, maps, and vibration

Voice or video call setup uses short-lived signaling through the Ba server and the device microphone/camera only while a call is in progress.

Where map tiles are fetched through the Ba server from map providers (for example street and satellite tile sources). The app may send tile coordinates needed to draw the map. This is not used to build an advertising profile.

Poke uses the device vibrator (haptics). No poke sound or banner is sent.

Health-related notes

Periods and Meprate logs are part of the encrypted shared journal. They are only for the couple’s own tracking. They are not medical advice and are not sent to health platforms.

Device identifiers

Ba keeps a local device id so phones in the room can be told apart for sessions, the active Where pin, and optional notifications. This is not used for advertising.

How long data is kept

Encrypted room content stays while the shared room exists. Sessions expire after inactivity. Location pins for a device are dropped when that session ends or sharing is turned off. If the hosting service is reset without durable storage, server-side data may be lost; keep copies of anything important outside the app if you need them.

Account deletion

In the app: Settings → Delete account, then enter the room code in the verification field and tap Delete. The server checks that code before closing the room. That removes your login for this side and closes the shared room for the couple.

You can also use the delete account request page or email tudus0602@gmail.com.

Children

Ba is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when features or hosting change. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
