Full description
  Ba is a quiet shared space for one couple.

  Open a private room with an invite code, then keep chat, routines, daily notes,
  to-dos, memories, periods, family notes, and optional location sharing in one
  place. Shared content is encrypted on your device before it leaves the phone.

  There are no ads and no public profiles. Location is optional and only used
  when you turn on Share location for Where.
